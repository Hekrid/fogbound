# Fogbound Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

Fogbound is a fan-made app for Dead by Daylight communities on Root. It is not made, endorsed or
sponsored by Behaviour Interactive. Dead by Daylight and its artwork belong to Behaviour Interactive.

This page explains what Fogbound keeps, why, and for how long.

## What Fogbound stores

Each community that adds Fogbound gets its own separate storage, kept on Root's servers. Nothing is
shared between communities, and nothing is sold or used for ads.

| What | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings chosen by the community's admins (channels, roles, options) | So the app works the way the community set it up | Until the admins change them or remove the app |
| Looking-for-group posts: who posted, who joined, the options picked and the note | To show and announce open groups | Deleted 7 days after the group closes |
| Game nights: who planned it, the title, note, time and who signed up | To show the event and send the reminder | Deleted 14 days after the start time |
| Shrine wishlist: the perks a member wants | To let that member know when one is in the Shrine of Secrets | Until the member removes them |
| Saved builds and likes | To keep a member's builds and show shared ones to the community | Until the member deletes the build |
| A linked Steam account: Steam ID, Steam name and avatar link | To show the member's Dead by Daylight stats | Until the member (or an admin) unlinks it |
| Leaderboard numbers for linked members (hours played, bloodpoints, escapes, sacrifices, generators) | For the community leaderboard | Deleted when the account is unlinked |
| Game data and pictures (perks, characters, Shrine, news) | So pages load quickly | Refreshed regularly; contains no personal data |

Fogbound uses Root member IDs and display names to show who posted or joined something. It does
not read or store private messages, and it does not store passwords or Steam sign-ins. Linking
Steam only needs a public profile link or ID.

## Outside services

- **Steam** (Valve): public profile pages, public Dead by Daylight stats and game news. Your
  avatar picture is loaded from Steam when your stats are shown.
- **dbd.tricky.lol** (a community fan site): perk and character data, the Shrine of Secrets,
  pictures, and public player stats looked up by Steam ID only.

Fogbound only sends these services what is needed to look something up (for example a Steam ID).
No Root account details are sent to them. Their own privacy policies apply to their sites.

## Your choices

- Unlink Steam at any time in the Stats tab. Your Steam details and leaderboard numbers are deleted
  right away.
- Remove wishlist perks, leave groups or game nights, and delete your saved builds at any time.
- Community admins can unlink members and change or turn off any feature in Settings.
- If a community removes Fogbound, its data is no longer used.

## Developer access

The app's developer can see technical information about a community where Fogbound is installed
(such as settings, version and counts of posts or linked members) to fix problems. This is not
shared with anyone.

## Children

Fogbound follows Root's own age rules and does not knowingly collect extra information about
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
